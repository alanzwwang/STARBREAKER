A fast space shooter that runs in your browser. Fly, aim, dodge, and pick upgrades to survive.

## 🎮 Controls

- **W A S D** or **arrow keys** to fly
- **Mouse** to aim (your ship fires automatically)
- **SPACE**, **SHIFT** or **right-click** to dash. You're invincible in the middle of a dash!
- On a touchscreen: left thumb flies, right thumb aims (or use auto-aim), plus a **DASH** button

Your best score is saved in your browser.

## 🚀 Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No install needed. The whole game is in that one file.
